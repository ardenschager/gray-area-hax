chromagrain: instrument re-voice (v001)

Every built-in instrument playing its starter phrase at 120 bpm (repeated to at least 6 s), rendered by plyphon exactly like the app does (loop-folded, loudness-normalised to about -16 dBFS RMS), so before and after play at matching loudness. Drums are also soloed. Spectrograms are log-frequency. Stats come from tools/audio_check.py. "clicks" on acid are saw-wave edges and on the drone are the intentional tape-dust specks; hat "near-clip" is the soft-knee limiter catching a sparse solo hat (no hard clipping; the kit render peaks at -1.5 dBFS). See NOTES.md for what changed and why.
